About us

A logistics partner built around your growth

Splendid Freight International Ltd. was founded in 2015 with one intention: to provide exceptional, personalized logistics services that contribute to the growth and prosperity of our clients.

We provide comprehensive supply chain solutions across a wide range of industry sectors, and we are committed to efficient, secure logistics that meet the needs, wants and demands of every client.
